# Global Fashion Retail Analytics Dataset Overview

**Global Fashion Retail Analytics Dataset Overview**

This extensive dataset captures two years of detailed transactional data for a multinational fashion retailer, providing a rich foundation for sophisticated retail analytics, business intelligence, and financial analysis. With over **4 million sales records**, it offers deep insights into global retail performance, customer behavior, and multi-currency operations across major international markets.

---

## 1. Data Explanation and Scope

The dataset covers operations in **7 countries** and includes sales records from **35 distinct stores**. Its multi-country scope allows for direct comparison of retail performance across different economies and consumer landscapes.

### Key Geographic and Financial Dimensions:

* **Countries Covered (7):** United States (USD), China (CNY), Germany (EUR), United Kingdom (GBP), France (EUR), Spain (EUR), and Portugal (EUR).
* **Currencies:** Transactions are recorded in the local currencies of operation: **USD, EUR, CNY, and GBP**. This structure is critical for performing complex multi-currency analysis, including exchange rate effects and standardized cross-country financial reporting.

### Core Analytical Pillars:

| Analytical Pillar | Key Data Points & Applications |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sales & Geographic Comparison** | Daily, weekly, and seasonal sales trends; store-level revenue and volume; comparison of sales drivers across the 7 countries. |
| **Customer Behavior & Segmentation** | Demographic analysis (age, occupation), purchasing frequency, average basket size, and regional customer preferences, enabling robust customer segmentation (e.g., VIP, seasonal, value-driven). |
| **Product Trends & Performance** | Analysis of sales volume and revenue by product category (**Feminine, Masculine, Children**) and specific product attributes (size, color) to inform merchandising and inventory decisions. |
| **Pricing & Discount Analysis** | The impact of various pricing models and promotional discounts on sales volume and customer conversion across diverse geographic markets. |
| **Staffing & Operational Performance** | Data to evaluate store staffing ratios, analyze employee performance metrics, and correlate staff efficiency with store-level sales success. |
